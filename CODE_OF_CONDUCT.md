# Contributor Code of Conduct 📜

## Our Commitment

In order to foster an open and welcoming environment, we, as contributors and maintainers of **api-boilerplate**, commit to making participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sexual characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, friendly, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the technical quality of the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Respecting the privacy of other people's data, credentials, and repositories.
- Being explicit about trade-offs, limitations, and mistakes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Offensive, discriminatory comments or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing private information about others without explicit permission.
- Exposing credentials, `.env` contents, tokens, or personal data.
- Publishing real data collected with the scraper or using it to impersonate someone.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Enforcement and Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Reporting Violations

Instances of abusive behavior, harassment, or any unacceptable conduct may be reported privately to the maintainer through the official GitHub repository or through [@felipevenas](https://github.com/felipevenas). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and confidentially.

Do not use public issues to report a Code of Conduct violation or a security vulnerability.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews, and any official community channel associated with the project. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
